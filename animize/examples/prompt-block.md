# Prompt block

Paste the block below into a prompt that writes a short artifact where the
skill is not installed. It carries the skill's fidelity standard and a local
defect scan, and none of the procedure.

```text
Write the requested short artifact as deliberate prose. Lead with its point.
Preserve the substance of any source: actor, scope, polarity, modality,
chronology, causation, comparison, attribution, evaluative force, ownership,
and state. Reproduce identifiers, commands, paths, URLs, citations, titles,
quotations, numbers, dates, and units exactly.

Never invent memories, anecdotes, emotions, preferences, opinions, reactions,
relationships, credentials, quotations, sources, mistakes, slang, or personal
experience. Never add typos, casing tricks, or fake self-corrections.

State scope positively. Delete "this does not cover" scaffolding unless the
reader would rely on the omission. Keep real prohibitions and limits.

Keep judgment within its genre: reference prose states facts, options, limits,
and errors; how-to prose may recommend and warn; explanation and review prose
may judge with support. Keep source-owned stance at its supported strength.

When the artifact is technical, instructional, or reporting prose:
- pick one genre: how-to, reference, explanation, or tutorial
- address the reader as "you"; use present tense for current behavior
- prefer familiar precise words; keep necessary technical terms
- write instructions as positive imperatives with the condition first
- give each sentence one main action or relationship; keep a long sentence
  when splitting would hide cause, condition, sequence, or qualification
- name a clear subject and actor; use one term per thing
- prefer real symbols, files, flags, and commands in code font
- put "only" and "not" beside what they modify; give every pronoun one
  antecedent and every clause a verb
- make "and" and "or" grouping explicit; write plurals without "(s)"
- use sentence-case headings; number sequences; bullet other collections;
  introduce every list with a full sentence; keep items parallel

Draft once. Then scan for these local defects and repair only real ones:
em or en dashes (default: none; use a period, comma, colon, or rebuilt
clause); chatbot wrappers, acknowledgments, offers to do more, drafting
residue; an unsupported "As a [role], I" opener; filler such as "it is
important to note"; "simply", "just", or "easily" in an instruction; "not
just X, but Y" or "X rather than Y" where nobody held X; every "do not" and
"it is" spelled out in a message or email, or a message clipped shorter
than its writer would say it aloud; "the real X" staged as a revelation; a
trailing "-ing" clause that claims significance; a bold label that restates
its own line; two thoughts whose relationship needs backtracking; "this" or
"which" with no clear referent; a described symbol whose real name is
visible.

Keep a candidate that has a defensible function. Make the minimum repair.
Reread once for directness and rhythm. Return only the artifact, plus a flag
when a material source issue needs one.
```

The block omits everything the procedure adds: the content lock, the
Preserve regime, the skeptical-reader passes, verification, and the reference
catalogs. An invocation of the skill never substitutes this block for the
procedure.
