## Clear-communication lineage

Animize's quick path and clarity constraints synthesize these sources:

- [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) for distinguishing tutorials, how-to guides,
  reference, and explanation
- [Google developer documentation style
  guide](https://developers.google.com/style) for reader address, task-focused
  instructions, terminology, and accessible structure
- [ASD-STE100](https://asd-ste100.org/) for controlled sentence load, stable
  terminology, and unambiguous technical instructions
- John R. Kohl, _The Global English Style Guide_ (SAS Press), for
  single-reading syntax and globally accessible English
- [`technical-writing`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/46125561306434d8a1d7745d540d8932ab0cd2a2/pstack/skills/technical-writing/SKILL.md)
  (MIT), for organizing those standards as layered checks and preserving
  clarity when applying them

These sources guide expression and document form. They do not authorize a
change to source meaning, technical precision, or deliberate voice.
